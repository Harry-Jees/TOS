# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept good-faith feedback and prioritize the project's users and safety.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's credentials, screenshots, logs, or personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized abuse, deliberate disruption, or publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove content and may block contributors for behavior they consider abusive, threatening, or persistently disruptive. Report concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub.
